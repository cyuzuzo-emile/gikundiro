About Rayon Sports FC

Founded in 1968, Rayon Sports FC has been a symbol of excellence in Rwandan football for over 50 years.

Our History

Rayon Sports FC was founded in 1968 and has since become one of the most successful and popular football clubs in Rwanda. The club's name "Rayon" means "ray of light" in French, symbolizing hope and inspiration for Rwandan football.

Throughout its history, Rayon Sports has been a dominant force in Rwandan football, winning numerous domestic titles and representing Rwanda in continental competitions.

Our Mission

To develop and promote football talent in Rwanda while maintaining the highest standards of sportsmanship and professionalism. We aim to inspire the next generation of footballers and continue our legacy of success both on and off the field.

Our Values

  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in everything we do
  • Passion: Playing with heart and dedication
  • Unity: Working together as a team and community
  • Integrity: Upholding fair play and sportsmanship
  • Innovation: Embracing new ideas and approaches

Achievements & Trophies

  • Rwandan Premier League: 8 titles
  • Rwandan Cup: 10 titles
  • Rwandan Super Cup: 5 titles
  • CECAFA Club Cup: 2 titles
  • Continental: Multiple appearances in CAF Champions League and CAF Confederation Cup
  • Youth Development: Multiple youth league championships

Our Home: Kigali Pele Stadium

Stadium Information

Kigali Pele Stadium, located in the heart of Kigali, is the home ground of Rayon Sports FC. With a seating capacity of 15,000, it provides an electrifying atmosphere for every match.

Facilities

  • 15,000 seating capacity
  • Modern training facilities
  • Player locker rooms and medical center
  • VIP lounges and corporate boxes
  • Merchandise stores and concession stands
  • Accessibility features for disabled fans
